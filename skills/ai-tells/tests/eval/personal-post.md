I finally quit the band last Tuesday. Honestly? It wasn't about the music. It was about the 4am drives back from Scranton in Dave's van that smelled like wet dog and cigarettes.

Here's the thing nobody tells you about playing in a bar band for eleven years: it's not a hobby, it's a second job — one that pays you in free Yuengling and tinnitus. I loved it. I also hated it. Both are true and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

The last show was at the Blue Anchor. Twelve people came. My sister was three of them, somehow (she kept changing hats). We played "Rosalita" way too fast and nobody cared.

Ultimately, this journey taught me that endings are just beginnings in disguise. And that's the real lesson.
