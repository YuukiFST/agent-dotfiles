## Why Tidewater?

Tidewater isn't just a job queue — it's a complete paradigm shift in how teams think about background work. Built on a robust, battle-tested foundation, it seamlessly integrates with your existing stack, empowering developers to focus on what truly matters.

**Key Features:**
- **Retries:** Retries are handled automatically with exponential backoff.
- **Scheduling:** Scheduling lets you run jobs at a specific time.
- **Observability:** Observability is built in, ensuring you always know what's happening.

In today's fast-paced world, leveraging a reliable queue is crucial. Whether you're a startup or an enterprise, Tidewater serves as the backbone of your infrastructure. The future of background processing is here.
