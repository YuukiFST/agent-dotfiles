Run `../ui-polish/build.md` step by step, in its order, with these additions. Each addition lands in the step it names; nothing here replaces a step.

1. **Brief**: add a line for **waits and effect surfaces**: every async step with its estimated duration from recon step 7, and any voice, agent-avatar, generated-image, key-headline, badge or upsell-CTA surface.
2. **Libraries**: after the primitives, run the **combined gate** in `SKILL.md` on each wait and effect surface in the brief. Each survivor gets its Libraries.dev reference read in full now, so the markup is written around it; its install follows `effects.md` apply step 3.
3. **Structure**: the copy written here includes the label beside each orb, naming the real activity (combined gate step 5).
4. **Markup**: a loading state carries its chosen effect wired to the real flag, and the indicator it supersedes is never written.
5. **Polish pass**: effects are sized and radiused from tokens, with their labels optically aligned (combined gate step 4).
6. **Motion**: effects are motion too. Their entrance, exit and the reduced-motion cases their package leaves open are built here (combined gate steps 6 and 7).
7. **Review gate**: the Audit row of `SKILL.md` **Modes**: `ui-polish`'s audit from step 3, with the effects reviewer in the fan-out and the effect checks in the gate. A new screen ships only after its own audit returns no HIGH finding.
